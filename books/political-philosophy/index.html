Political Philosophy

A beginner's guide to Jonathan Wolff's An Introduction to Political Philosophy. Each lesson takes one question, explains the answers that have been given to it, and shows where the argument still breaks down.

Wolff, 4th edition · Oxford University Press, 2023 · written for readers with no prior philosophy

Every lesson has two versions — Standard and Explain simply — switchable at the top of the page. Read the lesson before the chapter, not after: it gives you the vocabulary, the situation, and the shape of each argument, so that opening Wolff feels like recognising material rather than meeting it cold.

The book

Wolff's book is deliberately organised by question rather than by chronology. It doesn't march from Plato to Rawls; it takes one problem at a time and shows you the strongest answers anyone has given. That means you can read chapters out of order — but each one assumes the vocabulary of the last, which is what these lessons supply.
