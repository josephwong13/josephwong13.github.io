The Quest

Frodo knew what he had to do. The Ring had to reach Mount Doom. It was not a choice — it was inevitable, the only path that could save Middle-earth. And so he was resolute.

Sam watched his friend grow thinner, paler, wearier with each step. It was a poignant sight — a small hobbit carrying the weight of the entire world. Yet Sam never stopped believing. He would help Frodo press on when every part of him wanted to stop.

The journey through Mordor tested every limit they had. There was no map, no hope of rescue. They had to see it through — because if they failed, there would be no one else to finish what they started.

Sam would often look at Frodo and feel a deep, poignant sadness. This was not the Frodo who had laughed in the Shire. The Quest had changed him — and Sam knew that even if they succeeded, some things could never go back to how they were.

Word 1 of 3
resolute
/ˈrɛz.ə.luːt/ · REZ-ə-loot · adjective · 堅決的，果斷的
Admirably determined and unwavering in purpose. A resolute person doesn't hesitate or waver.
↳ From the story: Frodo was resolute — he knew the Ring must reach Mount Doom, and there was no turning back.
💼 Work:She remained resolute in her decision to resign, even when her boss offered a promotion.
🗣️ Debate:His resolute defence of free speech never wavered, regardless of the audience.
🌍 Activism:The protesters were resolute, standing in the rain for hours without leaving.
Word 2 of 3
poignant
/ˈpɔɪ.njənt/ · POY-nyənt · adjective · 令人心酸的，深刻的
Evoking a keen sense of sadness or emotional depth. A poignant moment touches something deep inside you.
↳ From the story: Sam felt a poignant sadness watching Frodo — this was not the hobbit who had once laughed in the Shire.
🎬 Film:The final scene of the movie was poignant — not a dry eye remained in the theatre.
📖 Literature:The poem's poignant reflection on loss stays with readers long after they finish it.
💬 Conversation:His poignant remark about missed opportunities made everyone stop and think.
Word 3 of 3

/ɪnˈɛv.ɪ.tə.bəl/ · in-EV-i-tə-bəl · adjective · 不可避免的
Certain to happen; impossible to avoid or prevent.
