The Crown

The Ring's power was insidious. It did not attack openly — it whispered. It promised. It found the smallest cracks in a person's heart and grew there, quietly, until there was no room left for anything else.

Gandalf knew this. He refused to touch the Ring, even when Frodo offered it freely. His will was indomitable — centuries of wisdom had taught him that even the strongest could fall if they believed themselves immune.

The Fellowship's hope was tenuous at best. Every setback — the loss of Gandalf in Moria, Boromir's betrayal, the broken Fellowship — frayed it further. They were held together by little more than trust and a promise.

In the dark days, the Ring seemed to hold sway over all of Middle-earth. And yet the Fellowship's vow — that darkness would not win — had been spoken. And what was spoken would come to pass, one way or another.

📖 Words

Word 1 of 3
insidious
/ɪnˈsɪd.i.əs/ · in-SID-ee-əs · adjective · 暗中為害的，陰險的
Proceeding in a gradual, subtle way that causes harm without being noticed until it's too late.
↳ From the story: The Ring's power was insidious — it whispered promises and grew silently inside its bearer.
🦠 Health:Stress is an insidious health risk — it builds up slowly over years before causing serious damage.
💻 Tech:The malware was insidious, quietly stealing data for months before anyone detected it.
🏢 Culture:Gossip can be an insidious force in a workplace, eroding trust without anyone noticing.
Word 2 of 3
indomitable
/ɪnˈdɒm.ɪ.tə.bəl/ · in-DOM-i-tə-bəl · adjective · 不屈不撓的
Impossible to subdue, defeat, or discourage. An indomitable spirit cannot be broken.
↳ From the story: Gandalf's will was indomitable — centuries of wisdom had taught him to resist the Ring's pull.
🏅 Athlete:Her indomitable will to compete drove her to win gold after a career-threatening injury.
🌍 History:The indomitable spirit of the city's residents kept hope alive through years of siege.
👫 Personal:Despite every setback, his indomitable optimism never faded — he always believed things would improve.
Word 3 of 3
tenuous
/ˈtɛn.ju.əs/ · TEN-yoo-əs · adjective · 脆弱的，薄弱的
Very weak or slight; having little substance or strength. A tenuous connection could break at any moment.
↳ From the story: The Fellowship's hope was tenuous — held together by little more than trust.
📈 Business:The company's tenuous finances meant it could survive only a few more months without investment.
🤝 Relationships:Their tenuous alliance was based on mutual need, not trust — it could break at any moment.
📚 Logic:His argument was tenuous at best, relying on a single questionable assumption.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2
hold sway
To have dominating power, influence, or control over a person or situation.
· 佔據支配地位
↳ From the story: In the dark days, the Ring seemed to hold sway over all of Middle-earth.
🗳️ Politics:Fear held sway over the electorate, driving voters toward extreme policies.
🎨 Culture:Minimalism held sway in design for a decade before maximalism made a comeback.
Phrase 2 of 2
come to pass
To happen or occur — a literary or formal way of saying that something predicted or expected has happened.
· 發生，實現
↳ From the story: What was spoken would come to pass, one way or another — the Fellowship's vow was unbreakable.
📜 Prophecy:The ancient prophecy came to pass when the rightful heir finally returned to claim the throne.
💼 Work:What the analyst predicted last quarter has come to pass — the market has shifted exactly as forecast.
