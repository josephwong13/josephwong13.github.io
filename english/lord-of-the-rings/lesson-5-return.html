The Return

The Ring was destroyed. Sauron was gone. And yet the hobbits returning to the Shire found it changed — scarred by war, and by the profound absence of the innocence they had left behind.

Frodo had carried the Ring to its end, but the journey had marked him in ways that would never fully heal. He was not broken — he was resilient, still standing after everything — but some wounds run too deep.

Sam, on the other hand, returned with a quiet strength. He had seen the worst the world could offer and had not faltered. His love for the Shire was redemptive — it reminded everyone that even after the darkest night, life could bloom again.

It slowly began to dawn on the hobbits that they had survived — truly survived. The war was over. The Shadow had lifted. And as they rebuilt their homes and planted new gardens, they knew they had helped turn the tide for all of Middle-earth.

📖 Words

Word 1 of 3
resilient
/rɪˈzɪl.i.ənt/ · ri-ZIL-ee-ənt · adjective · 韌性強的，能迅速恢復的
Able to recover quickly from difficulty, adversity, or change. A resilient person bends but doesn't break.
↳ From the story: Frodo was resilient — still standing after everything, even though some wounds would never fully heal.
💼 Work:The company proved resilient, bouncing back from near-bankruptcy within two years.
🌱 Nature:After the wildfire, resilient shoots of green appeared through the blackened soil within weeks.
👫 Personal:Children are remarkably resilient — they can adapt to change faster than adults often give them credit for.
Word 2 of 3
profound
/prəˈfaʊnd/ · prə-FOWND · adjective · 深刻的，深遠的
Very great, intense, or deeply meaningful. A profound experience changes how you see the world.
↳ From the story: The hobbits felt a profound absence — the Shire was scarred by war and the innocence they had left behind.
📖 Wisdom:His profound observation about forgiveness stayed with me for days after our conversation.
💼 Work:The merger had a profound impact on the company culture, changing everything from hierarchy to daily routines.
🎵 Art:Listening to her play was a profound experience — the music seemed to speak directly to something deep inside.
Word 3 of 3
redemptive
/rɪˈdɛmp.tɪv/ · ri-DEMP-tiv · adjective · 救贖的，彌補的
Acting to make something bad better, to restore worth or meaning after hardship or failure.
↳ From the story: Sam's love for the Shire was redemptive — it reminded everyone that life could bloom again after darkness.
🎬 Film:The final act of the movie is a redemptive one — the villain sacrifices himself to save the hero.
💼 Work:His apology was genuinely redemptive — he didn't just say sorry, he changed his behaviour completely.
🙏 Personal:For many, helping others is a deeply redemptive act that gives meaning to their own struggles.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2
dawn on
To become gradually clear or understood — as when a realisation slowly reaches your consciousness.
· 逐漸明白，意識到
↳ From the story: It slowly began to dawn on the hobbits that they had survived — truly survived.
💬 Realisation:It dawned on her that she had been staring at the same page for twenty minutes without reading a word.
💼 Work:It dawned on the team that the solution had been right in front of them the whole time.
Phrase 2 of 2
turn the tide
To reverse the course of events, especially from a losing position to a winning one.
· 扭轉局勢
↳ From the story: By destroying the Ring, the hobbits helped turn the tide for all of Middle-earth.
⚽ Sports:The substitute's goal turned the tide of the match, bringing the team from 2-0 down to a stunning victory.
💼 Business:The new CEO's strategy turned the tide for the struggling company within a single quarter.
