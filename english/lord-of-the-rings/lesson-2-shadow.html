The Shadow

Mordor loomed large in the minds of the Fellowship — a dark ominous presence that seemed to grow closer with each day.

The Ringwraiths were relentless. They did not sleep. They did not tire. They followed the Ring with a single-mindedness that terrified even the bravest. No matter how far the hobbits ran, the shadow behind them never shrank.

Gollum followed them through the Dead Marshes — a treacherous guide who had once betrayed his own kin. The ground beneath their feet was treacherous too: bog that would swallow a man in seconds, false paths that led to nowhere.

Frodo could not stop thinking about the Ring. He would dwell on its power late into the night — what it could do, what it was doing to him. The others tried to keep his spirits up, but the shadow pressed in from all sides.

📖 Words

Word 1 of 3
ominous
/ˈɒm.ɪ.nəs/ · OM-in-əs · adjective · 不祥的，預兆不好的
Giving the impression that something bad or unpleasant is about to happen.
↳ From the story: Mordor was an ominous presence — a darkness that seemed to grow closer each day.
☁️ Weather:The sky turned an ominous shade of green before the tornado touched down.
💼 Work:His boss's silence was ominous — she usually had plenty to say after a quarterly review.
📰 News:The ominous headlines about the economy made everyone nervous about their savings.
Word 2 of 3
relentless
/rɪˈlɛnt.ləs/ · ri-LENT-ləs · adjective · 不懈的，無情的
Unyielding in intensity or pace; never stopping or giving up, often oppressively so.
↳ From the story: The Ringwraiths were relentless — they did not sleep or tire, following the Ring without pause.
💼 Work:The sales team's relentless pursuit of the target finally paid off in the last quarter.
☀️ Nature:The relentless summer heat dried up the reservoir and forced water rationing.
🎓 Learning:Her relentless practice — four hours every day — is what made her a concert pianist.
Word 3 of 3
treacherous
/ˈtrɛtʃ.ər.əs/ · TRECH-ər-əs · adjective · 背叛的，危險的
Guilty of betrayal; also: hazardous because of hidden dangers that can deceive or harm.
↳ From the story: Gollum was a treacherous guide — and the Dead Marshes were treacherous ground that could swallow them both.
🏔️ Travel:The mountain pass became treacherous after the snowfall, with ice hiding deep crevasses.
🤝 Trust:He was a treacherous ally who sold secrets to the competitor while pretending to be loyal.
🚗 Driving:Black ice made the highway treacherous — drivers were warned to stay home if possible.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2
loom large
(Of something unwelcome) To appear as an approaching threat or worry that dominates one's thoughts.
· 逼近，令人擔憂
↳ From the story: Mordor loomed large in the minds of the Fellowship — a darkness they could not escape.
💼 Work:The deadline loomed large as the team struggled to fix the final bugs.
💰 Finance:Student debt looms large for many graduates, affecting major life decisions for years.
Phrase 2 of 2
dwell on
To think constantly and at length about something — especially something negative or worrying.
· 沉溺於，反覆思考
↳ From the story: Frodo would dwell on the Ring's power late into the night, unable to stop thinking about its pull.
💬 Conversation:It's healthy to learn from mistakes, but don't dwell on them to the point of paralysis.
💼 Work:Instead of dwelling on the failed pitch, the team focused on what they could improve for the next one.
