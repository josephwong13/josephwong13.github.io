The Shadow

The Ringwraiths rode out of Minas Morgul. An ominous black tide against a sky that had no stars.

The hunt was relentless. They did not sleep. They did not tire. They did not stop to ask directions. The Fellowship could run, but never rest — and the gap between them and the pursuers grew no wider with every passing day.

Beyond the gates, the paths through the Dead Marshes were treacherous. Luminous faces of the drowned floated beneath the surface, luring travellers to step off the path and into the water. Only Gollum, snarling and miserable, could guide them through — and even he could not be trusted.

The threat of Mordor loomed large in every mind. The closer they came, the heavier the air, the darker the night. Frodo would dwell on the Ring's whisper long after the others had fallen asleep — listening, always listening, to what it promised.

Word 1 of 3
ominous
/ˈɒm.ɪ.nəs/ · OM-i-nəs · adjective · 不祥的，預示厄運的
Giving the impression that something bad or harmful is going to happen; foreboding.
↳ From the story: The Ringwraiths rode out — an ominous black tide against a sky with no stars.
🌦️ Weather:There was an ominous stillness in the air just before the tornado touched down.
💼 Work:The board meeting was ominous — three executives were asked to leave before the others even sat down.
📰 News:Dark clouds gathered overhead — an ominous sign that the storm was coming.
Word 2 of 3
relentless
/rɪˈlɛnt.ləs/ · ri-LENT-ləs · adjective · 不間斷的，無情的
Unyielding in intensity or pace; never stopping, never softening, never giving rest.
↳ From the story: The hunt was relentless — they did not sleep, did not tire, did not slow.
💼 Work:The reporter's relentless investigation uncovered corruption that went all the way to the top.
🌦️ Weather:The relentless rain lasted nine straight days, flooding the valley.
🏅 Sport:His relentless pressure on the serve forced three double-faults in a row.
Word 3 of 3
treacherous
/ˈtrɛtʃ.ər.əs/ · TRECH-ər-əs · adjective · 背叛的；表面安全實則危險的
Dangerously unstable or deceptive; appearing safe while hiding real danger beneath the surface.
↳ From the story: The Dead Marshes were treacherous — luminous faces lured travellers into the water.
🏔️ Travel:The mountain road was treacherous — one wrong move and you'd slide off the edge.
🤝 Relationships:He learned the hard way that his business partner was treacherous, quietly siphoning funds into a private account.
🌊 Nature:Coastal currents can be treacherous — calm at the surface, deadly underneath.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2
loom large
To appear as an approaching threat or worry that dominates thoughts and overshadows everything else.
· 逼近，造成巨大壓力
↳ From the story: The threat of Mordor loomed large in every mind as the Fellowship drew closer.
💼 Work:With the deadline looming large, the team finally stopped overthinking and just started building.
💬 Personal:The thought of moving abroad had been looming large in her mind for years.
Phrase 2 of 2
dwell on
To think or worry about something repeatedly, often to an unhealthy or unproductive extent.
· 沉溺於，反覆想著
↳ From the story: Frodo would dwell on the Ring's whisper long after the others had fallen asleep.
💬 Personal:After the argument, she couldn't stop dwelling on the one careless thing he'd said.
💼 Work:The team agreed not to dwell on the failed launch and focus on what they could fix next quarter.
