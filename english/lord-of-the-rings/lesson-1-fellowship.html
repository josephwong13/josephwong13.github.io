The Fellowship

Nine companions set out from Rivendell. None knew what lay ahead.

Frodo had accepted the Ring — a burden that grew heavier with every step. Yet he remained steadfast, never once turning back. The others watched in awe as this small hobbit carried what even the mighty could not.

Aragorn was a formidable presence — weathered, skilled, carrying the weight of a kingdom he did not yet claim. When the orcs attacked at the pass of Caradhras, he stood between them and the hobbits, and nothing moved him.

The journey across the Misty Mountains was arduous. Snow up to their chests. No path. Bitter cold that seeped into their bones. Yet they pushed forward, because stopping meant failing their steadfast friend.

They had sworn to bear the burden together. And in the darkest moments, when all seemed lost, they would stand together — and that was enough.

📖 Words

Word 1 of 3
steadfast
/ˈstɛd.fɑːst/ · STED-fast · adjective · 堅定不移的，忠誠的
Resolutely firm and unwavering in loyalty, purpose, or belief. A steadfast person does not waver even under pressure.
↳ From the story: Frobo remained steadfast, never once turning back — even as the Ring grew heavier with every step.
👫 Friendship:Through years of hardship, she remained a steadfast friend who never abandoned those she loved.
💼 Work:The team's steadfast commitment to quality meant they never shipped a product they weren't proud of.
💬 Belief:He was steadfast in his principles, even when it cost him his job.
Word 2 of 3
formidable
/ˈfɔː.mɪ.də.bəl/ · FOR-mid-ə-bəl · adjective · 令人敬畏的，強大的
Inspiring fear or respect through being impressively powerful, capable, or intense.
↳ From the story: Aragorn was a formidable presence — weathered, skilled, and unstoppable in battle.
💼 Work:Her formidable reputation meant competitors knew better than to underestimate her.
🗳️ Politics:The senator is a formidable debater — she can dismantle any argument in under a minute.
🎵 Art:Beethoven's Ninth is a formidable piece that challenges even the most accomplished orchestras.
Word 3 of 3
arduous
/ˈɑː.dʒu.əs/ · AR-djoo-əs · adjective · 艱巨的，費力的
Involving great effort, difficulty, or hardship. An arduous task tests your endurance and will.
↳ From the story: The journey across the Misty Mountains was arduous — snow, cold, and no path to follow.
💼 Work:The audit was an arduous process that took three months and countless late nights.
🎓 Learning:Learning classical Chinese is arduous, but the ability to read ancient texts makes it worthwhile.
🏔️ Travel:The trek to base camp was arduous — steep climbs at high altitude with limited oxygen.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2
bear the burden
To carry a heavy responsibility or difficult duty — especially one that cannot be shared.
· 承擔重擔
↳ From the story: The Fellowship swore to bear the burden together, even though only Frodo could carry the Ring itself.
💼 Work:The CEO bears the burden of every layoff decision, even when the numbers leave no choice.
👫 Family:As the eldest child, she often bears the burden of caring for their aging parents alone.
Phrase 2 of 2
stand together
To remain united in support of each other, especially during difficult times.
· 團結一致，共同進退
↳ From the story: In the darkest moments, the Fellowship would stand together — and that alone gave them strength.
🌍 Society:In times of crisis, communities that stand together recover faster than those that fracture.
💼 Work:The team stood together when the project was blamed for the budget overrun, refusing to let one person take the fall.
