Listening & Understanding

A design critique. The room was gathered to review Mia's first major project.

Nina noticed Mia's hands were trembling slightly as she pulled up the slides. She started with a warm nod: "You look nervous — that's totally normal. Let's take this slow." She asked questions that helped Mia explain her own thinking rather than putting her on the defensive.

Tom scrolled through his phone while Mia presented. When asked for feedback, he waved his hand: "It's fine. Ship it." He didn't engage — not because he disliked it, but because he'd already decided it wasn't worth his attention.

Leila leaned forward. She listened without interrupting. When Mia finished, she said: "I love the layout — what if we tried a bolder colour on the call-to-action?" She took Mia's idea and built on it, treating it as a starting point.

Nina saw what Mia needed. Tom wrote her off. Leila made her better.

📖 Words

Word 1 of 3
perceptive
/pəˈsɛp.tɪv/ · pər-SEP-tiv · adjective · 敏銳的，觀察力強的
Quick to notice, understand, or judge things — especially things that are not obvious. A perceptive person picks up on what others miss.
↳ Back to the story: Nina was perceptive — she noticed Mia's hands trembling and adjusted her approach before saying a word.
📚 Literature:A perceptive reader notices the symbolism in the first chapter that everyone else misses until the end.
💼 Work:She's a perceptive manager who knows her team is burning out before anyone says a word.
👫 Personal:He made a perceptive observation about why I was upset — before I'd even opened my mouth.
Word 2 of 3
dismissive
/dɪsˈmɪs.ɪv/ · diss-MISS-iv · adjective · 輕視的，不屑一顧的
Showing that you think someone or something is not important or worth serious consideration. A dismissive attitude shuts down conversation.
↳ Back to the story: Tom was dismissive — "It's fine. Ship it." He didn't engage because he'd already decided it wasn't worth his time.
💼 Meeting:His dismissive wave told the whole room that their opinions didn't matter.
🗳️ Politics:The government was dismissive of the protestors' concerns, calling them "a vocal minority."
🏠 Personal:She was dismissive of my suggestion before I'd even finished the sentence — "That won't work."
Word 3 of 3
receptive
/rɪˈsɛp.tɪv/ · ri-SEP-tiv · adjective · 願意接受的，開放接納的
Willing to listen to, consider, or accept new ideas, suggestions, or feedback. A receptive person doesn't just hear — they engage and build on what you say.
↳ Back to the story: Leila was receptive — she listened carefully and then built on Mia's idea instead of dismissing or rewriting it.
💼 Work:The team was surprisingly receptive to the new workflow after seeing how much time it saved in the pilot.
🎓 Learning:Students are more receptive to feedback when it's delivered constructively, not as criticism.
🌍 Culture:The company is receptive to feedback from all levels — junior employees can email the CEO directly.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2
lend an ear
To listen carefully and sympathetically to someone, especially when they need to talk about a problem.
· 傾聽，聆聽
↳ Back to the story: After the critique, Nina stayed behind to lend an ear to Mia, who was feeling overwhelmed by the mixed feedback.
👫 Personal:Whenever I'm stressed about work, my best friend lends an ear without judging or giving unsolicited advice.
💼 Work:The best managers know when to stop solving problems and just lend an ear to their team.
Phrase 2 of 2
on the same page
To be in agreement or have a shared understanding about something. Often used to check alignment before moving forward.
· 達成共識，想法一致
↳ Back to the story: Nina and Leila made sure they were on the same page about the next steps before the meeting ended.
💼 Work:Before we start the sprint, let's get on the same page about priorities — otherwise we'll pull in different directions.
👫 Personal:My partner and I aren't on the same page about where to live, so we're taking a month to discuss it properly.
