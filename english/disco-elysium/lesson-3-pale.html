The Pale

On the edge of the world, there is a wall of white nothing. The Pale. It is not a place — it is an absence of place. Time behaves differently inside it. People who traverse it lose years, memories, sometimes themselves.

Joyce Messier, the harbour boss, explains it to Harry on the pier. The world is shrinking. Everything is passing away. The Pale is the erasure — and it grows a little wider every decade. The moments we cherish are ephemeral, here and then gone, swallowed by white.

Inside the Pale, reality recedes into formlessness. There is no up or down, no before or after. The Pale Driver sits at the helm of a boat that crosses this void — a woman who has seen the end of the world and kept driving. She speaks of things that have no shape, no name. The Pale gives them to her in whispers.

The world drifts toward oblivion — you cannot feel it happening, any more than you can feel the planet spin. But the fissures are real. The Pale blurs the line between reality and nothingness, memory and imagination. And for every soul in Revachol, everything — their homes, their history, their future — hangs in the balance.

Word 1 of 3
ephemeral
/ɪˈfem.ər.əl/ · i-FEM-ər-əl · adjective · 短暫的，轉瞬即逝的
Lasting for a very short time; fleeting and transient, like a single beautiful sunset or a butterfly's lifespan.
↳ From the story: The moments we cherish are ephemeral, here and then gone, swallowed by white nothing.
🌸 Nature:Cherry blossoms are famously ephemeral — they peak for only three days before the petals scatter in the wind.
💼 Work:The startup's success proved ephemeral — after one breakout quarter, the market shifted and revenue vanished.
📱 Tech:Social media fame is ephemeral; today's viral star is forgotten by next week.

/rɪˈsiːd/ · ri-SEED · verb · 後退，消退
To move back or away from a previous position, limit, or point; to diminish gradually over time.

💼 Work:The proposal was too recede — it described a vague concept without concrete objectives, budget, or timeline.
🎵 Art:The sculpture was an recede mass of melted metal, inviting every viewer to see a different shape in it.
🎓 Learning:His understanding of the topic remained recede — he could sense the shape of the idea but not articulate it.
Word 3 of 3
oblivion
/əˈblɪv.i.ən/ · ə-BLIV-ee-ən · noun · 遺忘，虛無
The state of being completely forgotten or unaware; utter nothingness, often with a sense of erasure from existence or memory.
↳ From the story: The world drifts toward oblivion — you cannot feel it happening, any more than you can feel the planet spin.
🔬 Science:Continental drift is oblivion — entire empires have vanished without a trace, their secrets lost forever.

❤️ Personal:His name had slipped into oblivion — no one remembered the man who once ran the city — no single fight, just a thousand unsent text messages.
