Revolution

Revachol was not always a broken place under Coalition occupation. There was a revolution once — a brief, bloody uprising that promised a new world. It failed, as revolutions often do. But its ghosts still walk the streets.

The air in Martinaise is thick with incendiary rhetoric. Evrart Claire speaks of solidarity from behind his desk at the docks. Measurehead spouts racial theory from his shipping container. Cuno shouts profanity from a half-collapsed building. Every corner of this village has someone preaching a doctrine of fire.

Then there is Korty, the old drunkard in the cafeteria. He was there — thirty years ago, at the barricade. His comrades are dead or in prison. The Coalition chose to retaliate with overwhelming force, punishing not just the fighters but the entire district. They made an example of Revachol.

The brutal suppression of the uprising left scars across the city. Even now, decades later, the old revolutionary sits at the cafeteria with his hands shaking around a bottle of cheap wine, he believes — with the terrible, stubborn faith of those who have nothing left to lose — that the dream was worth dying for.

The workers of Martinaise bear the brunt of the Coalition's occupation. They live in poverty while the rich behind the fence in Le Caillou sip champagne overlooking the slums. Harry must understand this history, feel the weight of it. He must decide where he stands — draw a line in the sand not just as a detective, but as a human being in a world that has never been fair.

Designed to cause fires, conflict, or strong emotion — used literally for flammable devices or figuratively for speech and writing that stirs up anger or trouble.

Word 2 of 3
retaliate
/vɪnˈdɪk.tɪv/ · vin-DIK-tiv · adjective · 報復性的，懷恨在心的
Having or showing a strong, sometimes irrational, desire for revenge; spiteful and punitive beyond what is necessary.
↳ From the story: The Coalition chose to retaliate with overwhelming force, punishing not just the fighters but the entire district.
💼 Work:After being passed over for promotion, he decided to retaliate — sabotaging his colleague's projects and spreading rumours.
⚖️ Law:When the company sued, the defendant retaliated rather than legitimate, noting the plaintiff had filed it only after losing a separate case.

Word 3 of 3
suppression
/səˈpreʃ.ən/ · sə-PRESH-ən · noun · 鎮壓，抑制
The act of putting an end to something by force; the violent, authoritative crushing of resistance, rebellion, or dissent.
↳ From the story: The brutal suppression of the uprising left scars across the city that never fully healed.
💼 Work:The regime’s suppression of dissent for safety guarantees, even when the factory threatened to relocate overseas.
