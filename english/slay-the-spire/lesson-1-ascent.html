The Ascent

You wake at the base of the Spire. A mysterious whale, Neow, offers you a gift — an ancient relic to aid your climb. You have nothing but a basic deck of strikes and defends.

Every floor you ascend tests your resolve. You battle Cultists, Sentries, and the occasional elite — each victory rewards you with new cards, gold, or potions. You learn to forge your deck carefully, choosing only the cards that strengthen your strategy.

At campfires, you face a choice: rest to recover lost health, or forge a card at the smith to make it permanently stronger — a calculated risk. Do you start from scratch, building something precise, or do you take every reward offered?

Whatever you choose, you must forge ahead. The Spire waits for no one.

📖 Words

Word 1 of 3
ascend
/əˈsɛnd/ · ə-SEND · verb · 攀登；上升
To climb or move upward; to rise to a higher level or rank. In the game, each successful run unlocks higher Ascension levels.
↳ From the story: Every floor you ascend tests your resolve — the higher you climb, the greater the challenge.
💼 Work:She ascended through the ranks over a decade to become the company's first female CEO.
🏔️ Travel:The climbers ascended the final 500 meters at dawn, reaching the summit as the sun rose.
📈 Growth:With each successful project, his reputation ascended to new heights in the industry.
Word 2 of 3
relic
/ˈrɛl.ɪk/ · REL-ik · noun · 遺物；聖物
An object surviving from an earlier time, especially one of historical or sentimental value. Relics in Slay the Spire provide powerful passive bonuses throughout your run.
↳ From the story: Neow gifted you an ancient relic — a small charm that would grow stronger with every battle won.
🏛️ History:The museum's newest exhibit features relics from the Ming Dynasty recovered from a shipwreck.
🎮 Gaming:That family board game has become a cherished relic — its pieces worn smooth by decades of play.
💬 Personal:My grandfather's pocket watch is the only relic I have left of him.
Word 3 of 3
forge
/fɔːdʒ/ · FORJ · verb · 鍛造；打造；開闢
To shape or create something with great effort — originally metal by heating and hammering, but now broadly: to build, to establish, or to move ahead with determination.
↳ From the story: At the campfire, you could rest — or forge a card at the smith, making it permanently stronger.
🤝 Relationships:They forged a deep friendship during those long nights working on the startup together.
💼 Work:The two companies forged a partnership that reshaped the entire industry.
🛤️ Life:She left her hometown at eighteen and forged her own path, against everyone's advice.

💬 Phrases

Phrase 1 of 2

To begin again from the very beginning, with nothing prepared or carried over.
· 從零開始，從頭再來
↳ From the story: Every run starts from scratch — only your knowledge carries over from your last attempt.
💼 Work:After the acquisition fell through, we had to start from scratch on the entire proposal.
🍳 Daily life:I burned the cake so badly I had to start from scratch — new batter, new pan, everything.
Phrase 2 of 2
forge ahead
To continue moving forward with determination, especially when progress is difficult.
· 奮勇前進，毅然前行
↳ From the story: Whatever you choose, you must forge ahead — the Spire waits for no one.
💼 Work:Despite losing their biggest client, the team decided to forge ahead with the new product launch.
🏃 Sport:Exhausted but unwilling to quit, the marathon runner forged ahead through the final kilometers.
