The Deck

After every battle, you're offered a choice: add one of three cards to your deck, or take none at all. This is where runs are won or lost.

Too many weak cards dilute your deck — you'll draw Strikes when you desperately need Blocks. The key is synergy: every card should work with the others. A card that applies Poison becomes devastating when paired with one that doubles Poison. This is how a deck transforms from random to unstoppable.

At the Merchant, you face a decisive moment. Do you buy that rare relic, or spend your gold removing a weak card? You must learn to weigh your options and sometimes cut your losses — selling a potion you'll never use to afford what you truly need.

Word 1 of 3
dilute
/daɪˈluːt/ · die-LOOT · verb · 稀釋；削弱
To make something weaker or less effective by adding other elements. A diluted deck has too many weak cards that reduce your chance of drawing the strong ones.
↳ From the story: Too many weak cards dilute your deck — you'll draw Strikes when you need Blocks.
💼 Work:Adding too many features will dilute the product's core value — users won't know what it's really for.

📊 Finance:Issuing more shares will dilute the value of existing shareholders' stakes.

/ˈsɪn.ə.dʒi/ · SIN-ər-jee · noun · 協同作用；增效
