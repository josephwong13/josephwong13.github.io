The Game in One Frame

Ark Nova is an
Action Efficiency Game

What You Control

  • How many actions per turn you spend
  • Which cards you keep or sell
  • When you break (pass first)
  • Map position & enclosure layout

What You Don't

  • The shuffle of the card deck
  • Your opponent's card draws
  • Conservation project placement slots
  • Break timing surprises

03

Map Selection

Which Map to Fight For

Tier 1 Silver Lake ⭐ Best map — no special ability means no setup. Open layout, easy money, extra action on bonus. Snowballs hard.
Tier 1 Outdoor Areas Broken ability: +2 capacity per adjacent building. Build a size-5 enclosure in ONE move. Free partnership zoo.
Tier 2 Research Institute Ignore one condition per animal. Free university bonus. Round-1 card upgrade possible with two universities.
Tier 2 Hollywood Hills Easy to cover 2 H's early. Great sponsorship synergies. Free partnership zoo.
Tier 3 Ice Cream Parlor / Commercial Harbor Playable but require specific setups. Commercial Harbor is the only map with a 2X spot.
Tier 4 Observation Tower / Park Restaurant Avoid. Awkward building positions, restricted zones, weak bonuses. You'll fight the map.
04

Your Only Weapons

The 5 Actions — Ranked

01

Association

Rush universities or partnership zoos. Getting your second of either = first card upgrade = first player advantage.

02

Cards

Upgrade early. More cards = more options = more synergy opportunities. Draw open, not blind.

03

Build

Upgrade early. Level 5 Build lets you create 4 enclosures at once. Essential for map bonus reach.

04

Animals

Upgrade for late-game power plays: multiple small animals at once, or very large animals (elephants, eagles).

05

Sponsors

Only upgrade if you have a specific synergy target. You can win without playing any sponsors.

05

The Best Action in the Game

Association Tactics

Partnership Zoos — Ranked

  • Asia — Best overall. Reduces animal costs, enables breeding.
  • Americas — Strong continent tag supply.
  • Africa — Good but less flexible.
  • Europe — Requires continent icon + partnership.
  • Australia — Weakest. Late-game only.

Universities — Ranked

  • 2 Reputation — Best. Push toward upgrade tier.
  • Hand Size +1 — Good for card-starved hands.
  • 2 Research Icons — Unlocks Science Museum, Medical Breakthrough sponsors.

Typical early sequence:
Zoo → Conservation Project → Zoo

Conservation Projects: Species Diversity (5 continents) & Animal Diversity (5 categories) are easiest to complete. Always check if your opponent benefits more before playing research.

Placement Matters More Than the Project

Conservation Project Bonuses

Play one by Round 2 for the 2 CP + upgrade. Placement slot value:

🏆 Snap / Build Income

Most powerful. Saves an action every round. Prioritize these slots.

+1 Worker

Take the first slot from your first project. Huge tempo boost.

+5 Money

Only valuable if you're spending it immediately that round.

+12 Money

Only worth taking if you have a large immediate purchase planned.

+3 X-tokens — best late-game when you have the resources to spend them. Not worth rushing early.

The Cards on the Table

Animal Selection Priority

1

Can you play it?

An animal you can't play before the endgame is a dead card.优先.

2

Does it advance goals?

Conservation projects or final scoring cards. Not just appeal.

3

Action utility?

Does its special action let you break the action-count equality?

4

Appeal only

If none of the above, play for Appeal. That's the baseline floor.

⚠️ The Elephant Trap

"In many games even one elephant will be too many. Don't shape your entire early game around one big animal from your starting hand."

Elephants need 7 money + a size-5 enclosure. That's a huge early investment. Meanwhile your opponent is building engines.

08

The Engine, Not the Fuel

Sponsor Synergy Logic

Sponsors are about fit, not power. Every sponsor asks: what does this fit with?

Sponsor Benefit Types

  • One-off tag bonuses — cash in when you play a type
  • Ongoing bonuses — scale with your engine
  • Extra buildings — map control + free enclosures
  • End-game bonuses — win-more if you're already winning

Key Question Per Card

  • Does this fit my conservation projects?
  • Does it tag my continent strengths?
  • Does it pair with my partner zoo?
  • Or is it just... a nice card?

You can win without playing any sponsors. Don't force a sponsor into your deck if it doesn't synergize — every wasted slot is an action lost.

09

Spatial Strategy

Build — The Underrated Action

Why Upgrade Build Early

  • Level 5 Build = 4 enclosures per action
  • Reach map bonus thresholds faster
  • Fill early kiosks for income
  • Fill isolated map spaces before opponent
  • Create enclosure chains for large animals

Watch Out

  • Size-2 enclosure income can block large animal placement — plan layout
  • Don't over-build enclosures you won't use
  • Map bonuses reward adjacent buildings — think in clusters, not lines
  • Late-game: running out of build slots = running out of game

If you're not careful late-game it is easy to really limit your opportunities to play large animals. Build the space first.

10

More Than a Pass

When to Take the
X-Token

Action Stuck at Slot 5

Sponsors is at 5 and you have no sponsor cards to play. X-token is better than wasting slot 5 on a bad action.

Moving Down Increases Value

Moving a valuable action down gets your best action sooner in the next round. Banking X-tokens helps achieve goals before break.

Banking for a Break

Save X-tokens to execute a big combo after your opponent passes. One well-timed X-token turn can flip the game.

Deny the Opponent

Sometimes the best X-token is to deny a strong opponent a slot they need. Watch their board — know what they're building toward.

11

Before You Pass — Check These

The Winning Checklist

💰 Money

Can I build at least one enclosure or play an animal this turn? If not — you passed too early.

🃏 Cards

Do I have 3 useful cards in hand? If hand is all dead weight — Cards action before passing.

👷 Workers

Have I exhausted Association benefits this round? Partner zoos and universities compound — use them.

⬆️ Upgrades

Is an upgrade within reach? First to upgrade Cards or Build often wins the tempo war.

🎯 Goals

Am I working on too many simultaneous goals? Limit to 2 active goals. Spread effort = win nothing.

🔒 Deny

What card is my opponent fishing for? Drawing cards can deny them a game-winning combo piece.

"The best Ark Nova players know when to break these rules." — experience is knowing when the checklist doesn't apply.
